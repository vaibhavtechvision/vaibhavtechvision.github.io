Welcome to Vaibhav Tech Vision

Vaibhav Tech Vision brings blind and visually impaired users closer to technology. This website collects tools, guides and learning material in one place, all made to work smoothly with screen readers like TalkBack and NVDA.

From simple text tools to audio resources, everything here is free and runs directly in your browser, with nothing to install and no account to create.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support the blind and visually impaired community by providing accessible technology, practical resources and clear guidance.

We work to reduce digital barriers, so that every user can handle everyday technology with confidence and independence.

Our Vision

We imagine a world where technology is accessible to everyone, regardless of visual ability, and where blind and visually impaired people can take part equally in education, work and daily life.

Through useful tools, honest guidance and continuous improvement, we want to help build a more inclusive digital world.

About This Website

Vaibhav Tech Vision is built to make technology easier for the blind and visually impaired community.

This website provides simple tools, guides and resources that help users understand and use technology more effectively. On our YouTube channel, we share practical tutorials about using mobile phones and computers with a screen reader.

We also plan to organise events and learning activities related to assistive technology. For updates, visit our Events page.

We offer courses designed with screen reader users in mind. You can explore our courses to build your skills.

If you face any technical problem, want to suggest a topic or share feedback, please contact us. You can also read more about us.
