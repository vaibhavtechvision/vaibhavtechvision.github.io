About Vaibhav Tech Vision

Welcome to the About page of Vaibhav Tech Vision. Here you can read about our initiative, our website and our founder. Use the headings on this page to move quickly from one section to another.

About Vaibhav Tech Vision

Vaibhav Tech Vision is an independent, non-commercial initiative that works to make technology easier for blind and visually impaired people. We believe that phones, computers and the internet should be simple to use for everyone, whether or not they can see the screen.

We do this by sharing video tutorials, building free online tools and gathering useful resources in one place. Everything we offer is free. We are not a registered organization, and we do not sell anything.

About Our Website

This website is the home of our work. It has free tools for text, PDF files, money and audio, and an AI assistant that answers questions in plain language. It also has information about our channels and a way to contact us. New tools and sections are added step by step.

The website is made with screen reader users in mind. Pages use clear headings, labelled buttons and forms, and strong colour contrast, and they can be used with a keyboard. Most tools run inside your own browser, so the text and files you use stay on your device. This website does not collect personal information.

About Our Channels

We share video tutorials for screen reader users on the Vaibhav Tech Vision YouTube channel. You can also follow our Telegram channel and our WhatsApp channel for updates.

About the Founder

Vaibhav Tech Vision was started by Vaibhav, a visually impaired technology enthusiast. He has been working in the technology field since 2020, when he started his YouTube channel. As a screen reader user himself, he knows how hard it can be to use new apps and websites without help, and he wants to make that journey easier for others in the blind community.
