About Vaibhav Tech Vision

Vaibhav Tech Vision is a free online platform made for blind and visually impaired users. It brings useful tools, resources and learning material together in one place, so that everyday digital tasks become quick and simple.

What This Website Offers

The website offers online tools that help with text, documents and audio. Every tool opens right in your browser, works with screen readers, and can be used completely with a keyboard. New tools and sections are added regularly.

Who This Website Is For

This website is for anyone who finds technology hard to use, especially blind and visually impaired users who rely on screen readers like TalkBack, NVDA or JAWS. Sighted users are welcome too. Everything here is free for everyone.

How This Website Is Built

Accessibility is the first rule of this website. Pages use clear headings, labelled buttons and forms, large click areas and strong colour contrast. Every page is tested with a keyboard and a screen reader before it is published.

Your Privacy

This website does not collect personal information. The tools work inside your own browser, which means the text you type or paste never leaves your device. There are no sign-up forms and no tracking.

Our YouTube Channel

We also share video tutorials on mobile and computer usage on the Vaibhav Tech Vision YouTube channel, made especially for screen reader users.
