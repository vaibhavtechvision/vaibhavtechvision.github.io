Privacy Policy

Updated 16 November 2026.

AI Assistant

When you press Send, your question is sent through our Cloudflare backend to an external AI service for an answer. The services currently used are Groq, Google Gemini and, if those are unavailable, OpenRouter. OpenRouter may route a request to a free model provider. Their own data policies apply. Free services may use submitted content to improve their products.

Do not enter passwords, identity numbers, bank details or other private information. Your question does not stay only on your device. We have not added a server-side conversation database, but hosting and AI providers may process or retain service logs under their own policies.

Saved notes and browser tools

Notes you save from the assistant are stored in this browser using local storage. You can delete them in My Saved Notes. Clearing browser data may also remove them. The text manipulation tool works locally in your browser.

Hosting and other websites

GitHub Pages hosts this site and Cloudflare runs its assistant backend. These services receive ordinary web request information. External links and the Share action may send you or your selected text to other services. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Review the service data policies before using the assistant for personal information.
