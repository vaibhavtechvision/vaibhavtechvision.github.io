Apps

These are well-known apps that work well with screen readers and are useful for blind and visually impaired users. We have no connection with the companies that make them; this is simply an honest list.

  • Google Lookout

    Points your phone camera at the world and speaks what it sees: text, documents, products, currency notes and scenes.

  • Seeing AI

    A free app from Microsoft that reads short text and documents aloud, describes photos and recognises people and products.

  • Be My Eyes

    Connects you with a sighted volunteer over a video call when you need quick help with something visual.

  • TalkBack

    The screen reader built into Android phones. It speaks everything on the screen so the phone can be used without looking at it.

We will keep adding more apps to this list. If you know a good accessible app, tell us on our Contact page.
