Remove White Background

Make the white background of a photo see-through. This is made for signatures and logos scanned or photographed on white paper: sign on white paper, take a photo, and this tool gives you a signature with a see-through background that you can put on any document.

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you choose here is uploaded anywhere.

Step 1: Choose your photo

Works best when the background is white or nearly white. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP photos work.

Step 2: Choose how much to remove

Step 3: Download the result

The result is a PNG file, which keeps the see-through background. Honest note: this tool removes light backgrounds only. To remove the background behind a person, use the Passport Size Photo section, which has a smarter tool for people.
