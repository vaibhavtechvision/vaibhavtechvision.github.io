DOCX to PDF and Reader

Open a Word document ending in .docx and read it right here, with any screen reader. You can also save it as a PDF or download it as plain text.

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you choose here is uploaded anywhere.

Save as PDF opens your browser's print window. In that window, choose "Save as PDF" as the printer and press Save. Older Word files ending in .doc (without the x) cannot be read; please save them as .docx first.
