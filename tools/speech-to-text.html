Speech to Text

Press the start button and speak. The tool types what you say. When you stop, you can copy the text or download it as a file.

This tool uses your browser's own speech service, so it works best in Google Chrome. On Chrome, what you say is processed by Google's speech service to turn it into text. Nothing is saved or used by this website.

Sorry: this browser does not offer speech typing. Please open this page in Google Chrome on your phone or computer and try again.
