Image Compressor

This free tool makes your photo fit an exact file size that you type, for example 20 KB, 50 KB or 1 MB. It is made for government forms, exam forms and job applications that say "photo must be less than 50 KB" and so on.

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you choose here is uploaded anywhere.

Your photo never leaves your device. Everything happens inside your browser. First the tool tries quality settings; if that is not enough, it gently makes the photo smaller until it fits. The final size and dimensions are read out, so you always know exactly what you got.

Step 1: Choose your photo

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP photos work.

Step 2: Type the size you want

For example: type 50 and choose KB when a form asks for a photo under 50 KB. The photo will come out at your size or just under it.

Step 3: Download the result

Please note: a photo can only be squeezed so far. If you ask for a very tiny size, like 5 KB for a big colourful photo, the tool will tell you honestly when the photo would become too unclear to use, and give you the smallest clear version it could make. To edit the photo first (crop, rotate, brightness), use the Image Editor section above. For passport photos, use the Passport Size Photo section on this page.
