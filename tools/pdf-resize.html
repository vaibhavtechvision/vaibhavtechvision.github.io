PDF Resize

This tool changes the page size of a PDF. You can fit every page into a standard paper size like A4, A5, A3, Letter or Legal, or make all pages bigger or smaller by a percentage. The content of your pages is scaled to fit, so the quality stays the same.

Choose a PDF file, pick the new size, and press Resize PDF. The file stays inside your browser; it is never uploaded anywhere.
