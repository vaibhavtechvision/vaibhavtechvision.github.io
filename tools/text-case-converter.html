Text Case Converter

This tool changes the letter case of your text. You can turn everything into capital letters or small letters, put a capital letter at the start of every word, or use normal sentence case.

Type or paste your text below, choose a conversion button, and the result appears in the same box. You can then copy the result or listen to it. Your text never leaves your device.
