Circle Crop (profile photo)

Cut your photo into a circle, or into a square with rounded corners. This is popular for profile photos on WhatsApp, Telegram and other apps. The part outside the circle becomes see-through (transparent).

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you choose here is uploaded anywhere.

Step 1: Choose your photo

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP photos work. The tool cuts a square from the centre of the photo, so place your face in the centre before using it.

Step 2: Choose the shape

Step 3: Download the result

The result is a PNG file, which keeps the see-through part. To move the cut away from the centre, first crop the photo in the Image Editor section above.
