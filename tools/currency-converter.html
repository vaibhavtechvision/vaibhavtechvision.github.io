Currency Converter

This tool changes an amount of money from one currency to another, for example Indian rupees to US dollars, euros or pounds.

Type the amount, choose the currency you have in "From" and the currency you want in "To", then press the Convert button. The result is read out by your screen reader. Use the Swap button to exchange the two currencies.

Type numbers only, for example 1500 or 99.50.

Result

Rates come from free public services (Frankfurter, and Open Exchange Rates API as a backup). The result is only a guide and may differ from the rate your bank or money changer gives. Only the currencies and the amount are sent to these services. Nothing else leaves your device.
