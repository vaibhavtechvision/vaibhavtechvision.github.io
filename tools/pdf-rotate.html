PDF Rotate

This tool turns pages of a PDF so they face the right way. You can rotate all pages, only odd pages, only even pages, or a range you choose, by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.

Choose a PDF file, pick which pages and how much to turn them, and press Rotate pages. The file stays inside your browser; it is never uploaded anywhere.
