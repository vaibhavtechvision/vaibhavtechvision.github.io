Image Editor

This free tool edits your photos right inside your browser. Your photo never leaves your device; nothing is uploaded to any server.

You can make the photo bigger or smaller, crop it, rotate or flip it, fix brightness and colours, add text, and save it as JPG, PNG or WebP. Every control works with a keyboard and a screen reader: you type numbers or choose options, then press a button. The result is read out after every change.

Related tools: Passport Size Photo makes a correct passport size photo with a white background, and Image Compressor makes a photo fit an exact file size like 20 KB.

Step 1: Choose your photo

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP photos work. The photo opens on your device only.
