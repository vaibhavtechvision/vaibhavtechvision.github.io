Text to PDF Converter

This tool turns text you type or paste into a PDF file that you can save or share. It works in Hindi, English and every other language, because the text is placed into the PDF as a clear picture of the page, so it always looks exactly right.

Type or paste your text, choose a letter size, and press Create PDF. Your text stays inside your browser; it is never uploaded anywhere.
