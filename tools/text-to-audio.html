Text Speaker

Type or paste any text, and the tool reads it out loud using the voices built into your phone or computer. You can choose the voice, the speed and the pitch.

Everything is spoken by your own device; your text is not sent anywhere. This tool speaks the text aloud; it cannot save the speech as a sound file.
