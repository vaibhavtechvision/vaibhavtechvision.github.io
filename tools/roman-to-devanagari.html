Roman to Indian Scripts Typing

Pick a language, then type the way it sounds, using English letters. The proper writing appears below as you type. For example, with Hindi chosen, typing namaste gives नमस्ते.

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere.

This tool does not yet write in the Arabic-based scripts, so Urdu, Kashmiri and Sindhi are not here, and the Ol Chiki and Meitei Mayek scripts are not covered yet either.

Show the typing guide

Type the sounds the way you would say them:

  • Vowels: a, aa, i, ii or ee, u, uu or oo, e, ai, o, au. Example: bhaarat has a long aa.
  • Letters: ka, kha, ga, gha, cha, chha, ja, jha, ta, tha, da, dha, na, pa, pha or fa, ba, bha, ma, ya, ra, la, va or wa, sha, sa, ha.
  • Hard letters, typed with capitals: Ta, Tha, Da, Dha, Na, Sha.
  • Two consonants together join up: school becomes a joined letter pair.
  • An n or m just before another consonant becomes a dot or bindu where the writing system has one: hindi becomes हिंदी in Devanagari and হিন্দি in Bengali.
  • Tamil has one letter for each sound group, so ka, kha, ga and gha all become க, and cha, ja become ச. That is how Tamil is written.
  • English words, numbers and punctuation pass through as they are.
