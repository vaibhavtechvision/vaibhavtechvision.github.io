Roman to Hindi Typing

Type Hindi the way it sounds, using English letters, and the Hindi writing appears below as you type. For example, type namaste and you get नमस्ते.

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere.

Show the typing guide

Type the sounds the way you would say them:

  • Vowels: a = अ, aa = आ, i = इ, ii or ee = ई, u = उ, uu or oo = ऊ, e = ए, ai = ऐ, o = ओ, au = औ
  • Letters: ka = क, kha = ख, ga = ग, gha = घ, cha = च, chha = छ, ja = ज, jha = झ, ta = त, tha = थ, da = द, dha = ध, na = न, pa = प, pha or fa = फ, ba = ब, bha = भ, ma = म, ya = य, ra = र, la = ल, va or wa = व, sha = श, sa = स, ha = ह
  • Hard letters, typed with capitals: Ta = ट, Tha = ठ, Da = ड, Dha = ढ, Na = ण, Sha = ष
  • Two consonants together join up: type school and you get स्छूल.
  • An n or m just before another consonant becomes a bindu: hindi becomes हिंदी, and shanti becomes शांति.
  • English words, numbers and punctuation pass through as they are.
