Documents Converter

Change documents from one format to another, right in your browser. Everything is free, and your files never leave your device: each tool reads the file on your phone or computer and gives you the converted file to download.

PDF and text

  • PDF to Text Converter

    Pull all the text out of a PDF and save it as a plain text file. Works on digital PDFs, not on scanned photos of pages.

    Open PDF to Text Converter

  • Text to PDF Converter

    Type or paste text and turn it into a neat PDF document.

    Open Text to PDF Converter

  • Image to PDF Converter

    Turn one or more photos into a single PDF file.

    Open Image to PDF Converter

  • PDF to Image Converter

    Save the pages of a PDF as image files.

    Open PDF to Image Converter

Word documents

  • Text to DOCX Converter

    Type or paste text and turn it into a real Word document.

    Open Text to DOCX Converter

  • DOCX to Text Converter

    Pull all the text out of a Word document, without formatting, and save it as a plain text file.

    Open DOCX to Text Converter

  • DOCX to Web Page Converter

    Turn a Word document into a web page file that keeps headings, lists and tables.

    Open DOCX to Web Page Converter

  • DOCX to PDF and Reader

    Read a Word document on any device, and print or save it as a PDF through your browser.

    Open DOCX to PDF and Reader

One honest note: turning a PDF back into an editable Word document (PDF to DOCX) does not work reliably without paid software, so we do not offer it. Everything above genuinely works in your browser.
