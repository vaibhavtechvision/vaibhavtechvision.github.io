AI Studio

Welcome to AI Studio, the home of simple and accessible AI tools on Vaibhav Tech Vision. Our first tool, the VTV Assistant (our AI assistant), is ready. Type any question in plain language and get a clear, simple answer. Press Listen to hear an AI voice. If the free AI voice is unavailable, your device voice is used instead.

AI voice is generated only when you press Listen. The same answer audio is reused while this page stays open. Free limits are shared by all visitors: Gemini currently allows 10 new audio requests per day. After that, free Fish Audio and free HeyGen voices share the OpenRouter free allowance of up to 50 requests per day, also used by backup chat. The HeyGen voice listing currently ends on 30 November 2026. Availability may be lower and providers can change when a free limit is reached.

Ask AI

Type your question below and press the Send button. The answer appears on this page and your screen reader reads it aloud automatically. Under every answer there is a Listen button and an Actions button. The Actions button opens a menu where you can copy the answer, share it, or save it to your notes. Saved notes are stored only on your own device.

Privacy: Questions you send are processed by external AI services through our server. Do not enter passwords, identity numbers, financial details or other sensitive information. Free AI services may use submitted text under their own data policies. Saved notes stay only in this browser. AI answers may be wrong; verify important facts.



My Saved Notes

Answers that you save with the Save to Notes action appear here. They stay only on this device, in this browser.
