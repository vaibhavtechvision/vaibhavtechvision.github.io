Text Encryptor and Decryptor

This tool locks your text with a password, turning it into a secret code that nobody can read without the same password. You can safely share the coded text with anyone, and they can unlock it on this same page using the password.

Type your text, choose a password, and press Lock text. To unlock a coded message, paste it in, type the same password and press Unlock text. Everything happens inside your browser. Your text and password never leave your device, and we cannot see or recover your password.
