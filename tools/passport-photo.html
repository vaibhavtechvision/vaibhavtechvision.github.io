Passport Size Photo Maker

This free tool turns any photo of you into a correct passport size photo, with the exact dimensions used for Indian passports, visas, exam forms and job applications. It runs fully inside your browser: your photo never leaves your device.

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you choose here is uploaded anywhere.

It cuts your photo to the exact size, can put a white background behind you automatically, and can keep the file under a size limit like 50 KB, which many forms ask for.

For the best result: take the photo facing the camera, in good daylight, against a plain light-coloured wall, with no shadow behind you. The automatic white background works well with such photos but may not be perfect around hair; it is made by a small AI model that runs inside your browser, not on any server. If the result is not good enough for an official document, take the photo again against a plain white wall and choose "Keep the original background".

Step 1: Choose your photo

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP photos work. A photo where you face the camera gives the best result.

Step 2: Choose the photo size

All sizes are made at 300 DPI, the quality required for printing. The exact pixel dimensions are always read out in the result.

Step 3: Frame your face

The tool cuts the centre of your photo to the passport shape. If your face is too small, increase the zoom. If the cut is not centred on your face, use the move numbers: positive numbers move your face right or down, negative numbers move it left or up.

Step 6: Check and download

If the framing is not right, change the zoom or move numbers above and press "Make my passport photo" again. There is no limit; try as many times as you like.

Honest limits: the automatic white background is very good with plain backgrounds, but it can leave small mistakes around hair or shadows, and it cannot remove objects behind you like doors or trees. Very strict authorities (for example some embassies) may still ask for a studio photo. To edit the photo first, use the Image Editor section above. To squeeze any photo to an exact file size, use the Image Compressor section on this page.
