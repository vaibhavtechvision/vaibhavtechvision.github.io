Text Cleaner

This tool tidies up messy text. It can remove email addresses, web links and phone numbers, and it can fix extra spaces and empty lines.

Type or paste your text, tick the things you want to remove, and press Clean text. You can copy the result, listen to it, or download it as a text file. Your text never leaves your device.
