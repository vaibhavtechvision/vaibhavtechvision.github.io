Image Editor

Edit your photo step by step below. When you are done, you can make it an exact file size in the Image Compressor section, or turn it into a passport photo in the Passport Size Photo section.

Everything happens on your own device. Nothing you choose here is uploaded anywhere.

Step 1: Choose your photo

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP photos work. The photo opens on your device only.

Step 2: Make bigger or smaller (resize)

Step 3: Crop (cut out one part)

The crop keeps the part of the photo inside a box. Left and Top say where the box starts, counted in pixels from the top-left corner of the photo. Width and Height say how big the box is.

Step 5: Brightness, contrast and colour

100 means no change. Higher is more, lower is less. Each time you press the button, the change is added to the current photo. Press Start over to return to the original.

Step 7: Add text to the photo

Step 8: Add a watermark (see-through text)

A watermark is see-through text on top of the photo, for example your name or website, so others do not misuse your photo.

Step 9: Blur or cover one part of the photo

Hide a face, a name or a number in the photo. Like the crop, Left and Top say where the part starts in pixels from the top-left corner, and Width and Height say how big it is.
