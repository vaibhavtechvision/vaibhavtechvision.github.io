PDF Merge

This tool joins two or more PDF files into one single PDF. You choose the files and the order, and the tool makes one combined PDF for you.

Choose your PDF files one after another, put them in the order you want with the Move up and Move down buttons, and press Merge PDFs. Your files stay inside your browser; they are never uploaded anywhere.
