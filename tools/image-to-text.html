Picture to Text Reader

Choose a picture of a printed page, a document photo, or a screenshot. The tool reads the writing in it and gives you the text to copy or download.

It works with printed English and Hindi. Handwriting is not read reliably. Clear, straight, well-lit pictures give the best results.

Everything happens on your own device. The picture is never uploaded anywhere. The first time you use the tool, it downloads the reading engine itself (a few megabytes) from a free library host, so the first read can take a little longer.
