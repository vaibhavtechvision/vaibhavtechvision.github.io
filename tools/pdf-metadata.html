PDF Details Editor

Every PDF keeps hidden details inside it, like its title, author, subject and keywords. These are also called metadata. This tool shows you those details and lets you change them, then saves a new PDF with your changes.

Choose a PDF file to see its current details, change what you want, and press Save with new details. The file stays inside your browser; it is never uploaded anywhere.
