Audio Editor

These free tools work on your audio files right inside your browser. Your file never leaves your device; nothing is uploaded to any server. Every control works with a keyboard and a screen reader: you type numbers or choose options, then press a button. The result is read out after every step. MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and most other common audio files work, and some tools can also take the sound out of a video file.

This one page has ten tools:

  • Audio Trimmer: keep only the part you need, or cut a part out of a recording.
  • Audio Merger: join two or more audio files into one, in the order you choose.
  • Audio Converter: change an audio file, or the sound of a video, into MP3 or WAV.
  • Volume Changer: make a recording louder or quieter.
  • Speed Changer: make audio faster or slower while the voice still sounds natural.
  • Voice Over Mixer: put your voice on top of background music, and the music turns itself down while you speak.
  • Multi-Track Mixer: play up to four audio files together, each with its own loudness.
  • Fade In and Fade Out: make audio start and end smoothly.
  • Audio Reverser: play a recording backwards.
  • Clear Phone Speaker: play a cleaning sound to push water or dust out of your phone speaker.

1. Audio Trimmer

Keep only the part of a recording you need, or remove a part from the middle. You type the times in seconds, so it works fully with a keyboard.

MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A and most audio files work. You can also choose a video to trim its sound.

2. Audio Merger

Join two or more audio files into one single file. After choosing your files you can change their order with the Move up and Move down buttons, and add a short silence between them.

Order of your files

    3. Audio Converter

    Change an audio file into MP3 or WAV. You can also choose a video file here: its sound will be taken out and saved as audio.

    4. Volume Changer

    Make a recording louder or quieter. For example, 50 makes it half as loud and 200 makes it twice as loud. There is also an option to make the recording as loud as possible without distortion.

    5. Speed Changer

    Make a recording faster or slower while voices still sound natural, without turning into chipmunks or monsters. For example, 150 plays the recording in two thirds of the time. The first time you use this tool, your browser downloads a small audio engine (about 30 MB) once; after that it works straight away.

    6. Voice Over Mixer

    Put your voice recording on top of background music. The music automatically becomes quieter while your voice plays, and louder again when your voice ends. This is perfect for making a voice over for a video or a presentation.

    If the music is shorter than the voice, it starts again from the beginning. The result is as long as the longer of the two files.

    7. Multi-Track Mixer

    Play up to four audio files together, all starting at the same time, each with its own loudness. The result is as long as the longest file.

    8. Fade In and Fade Out

    Make a recording start and end smoothly instead of beginning or stopping suddenly.

    9. Audio Reverser

    Turn a recording around so it plays backwards, from the end to the beginning.

    10. Clear Phone Speaker

    If water or dust has entered your phone's speaker and the sound feels muffled, this tool plays a special cleaning tone that shakes the speaker and pushes the water or dust out. Hold the phone with the speaker facing down while it plays.

    Warning: the cleaning sound is loud. Do not hold the phone near your ear while it plays.
