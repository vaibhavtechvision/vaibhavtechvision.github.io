Government Circulars

Download official laws, circulars and accessibility guidelines useful to blind and low-vision people in India. All PDF files below are hosted on this website. The issuing department is credited in plain text.

This is not a government website. These are unchanged copies of official PDFs, not documents issued by Vaibhav Tech Vision. Our short English descriptions are only reading aids, not legal advice. Read the full document and any later orders before relying on a rule or applying for a benefit.

Sources checked: 16 November 2026. Government PDFs may contain Hindi as well as English. Some are scanned and may need OCR or assistance with a screen reader. The page descriptions are available as accessible web text.

Disability rights law

Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016

Issuer: Government of India, Ministry of Law and Justice. Act 49 of 2016; assent on 27 December 2016. Official copy supplied by the Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD).

The main disability-rights law covers equality, non-discrimination, education, employment and accessibility. Sections 16-17 concern education, section 20 concerns employment, and sections 40-46 concern accessibility. Some provisions apply specifically to persons with benchmark disabilities. Check the conditions of each provision.

Download the RPwD Act 2016 PDF

Banking and digital payments

Accessible digital payment systems: RBI guidelines

Issuer: Reserve Bank of India. RBI/2024-25/83, 11 October 2024; updated 13 October 2026.

Banks and authorised non-bank payment providers are asked to review their payment systems and devices for accessibility and make needed changes without weakening security. This includes devices such as point-of-sale machines.

Download RBI digital payment accessibility guidelines, updated 2026

Accessibility Standards and Guidelines for Banking Sector

Issuer: Government of India, DEPwD; banking standards of the Ministry of Finance. Gazette notification G.S.R. 361(E), 2 July 2024.

Standards cover bank branches, ATMs, websites and banking services. They include measures relevant to blind customers, such as accessible websites, tactile or Braille information and assistance.

Download banking accessibility standards 2024 PDF

Banking facilities to the visually challenged: historical 2008 circular

Issuer: Reserve Bank of India. RBI/2007-08/358, 4 June 2008. Addressed to scheduled commercial banks, excluding regional rural banks.

This historical circular says that facilities such as cheque books, ATMs, internet banking and lockers must not be denied because a person is visually impaired. Use it as background. For a present-day complaint or request, check the latest instructions applicable to the bank, including later consolidated directions.

Download the original RBI banking circular of 4 June 2008

Education and digital accessibility

Accessibility guidelines for higher education institutions and universities

Issuer: Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education; notified by DEPwD on 25 June 2024 through G.S.R. 358(E).

Guidelines cover inclusive learning, accessible teaching material, digital resources, campus facilities, libraries, assessments and examinations. They can help students and institutions understand accessibility measures and support needs.

Download higher education accessibility guidelines PDF

Accessibility for ICT products and services

Issuer: DEPwD and Bureau of Indian Standards. RPwD Amendment Rules notification dated 10 May 2023, with ICT accessibility standards.

ICT means information and communication technology. These standards cover accessibility of digital products and services, including requirements relevant to screen-reader and keyboard users. Read the notification together with the standards.

Download the ICT accessibility notification and standards PDF

Examinations and scribes

Check applicability carefully. The revised guidelines of 1 August 2025 were put on hold. The June 2026 order extended this to 31 December 2026; the July modification adds "or until further orders, whichever is earlier". Read the two 2026 orders together and check the notice for your examination. These copies are not a promise that no later order exists.

Extension order dated 25 June 2026

Issuer: DEPwD, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment. Refers to continuing the 2018 guidelines, their subsequent corrigendum and the 2022 guidelines for the examinations covered by the order.

Download the examination guidelines extension order, 25 June 2026

Partial modification dated 1 July 2026

Issuer: DEPwD. Read with the 25 June 2026 order. This PDF is dated 1 July; the official collection lists it on 2 July 2026.

Download the partial modification order, 1 July 2026

Written examination guidelines dated 29 August 2018

Issuer: DEPwD. Guidelines concerning persons with benchmark disabilities, including examination assistance. Read with applicable corrigenda and later orders. This official PDF is scanned and may not read properly with a screen reader.

Download the 2018 written examination guidelines PDF

Written examination guidelines dated 10 August 2022

Issuer: DEPwD. Concerns people covered by section 2(s) of the RPwD Act who have less than 40 percent disability and difficulty writing, subject to the prescribed certification and conditions.
