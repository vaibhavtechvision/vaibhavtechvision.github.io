Government Circulars

This page provides important circulars, guidelines and laws by the Government of India and the Reserve Bank of India for persons with disabilities. It helps visually impaired users stay informed about their rights and benefits. You can download any circular or guideline you want, in the form of a PDF. All PDF files are hosted on this website.

This is not a government website. These are unchanged copies of official PDFs, not documents issued by Vaibhav Tech Vision. Read the full document and any later orders before relying on a rule or applying for a benefit. Sources checked: 16 November 2026. Some PDFs are scanned and may need OCR or assistance with a screen reader.
