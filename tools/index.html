All Tools

Here are all the tools and sections available on Vaibhav Tech Vision. Everything is free and fully accessible with a screen reader and keyboard. Some sections are still being built, and they are honestly marked as coming soon. Choose a section below to open it.

  • Text Manipulation

    Change letter case, count words and characters, clean messy text and lock your text with a password.

    Open Text Manipulation

  • Text Case Converter

    Change your text to capital letters, small letters, title case or sentence case with one button. You can also listen to the result or copy it.

    Open Text Case Converter

  • Text Analyser

    Find out how many characters, words and lines your text has, and see which word appears the most.

    Open Text Analyser

  • Text Cleaner

    Remove email addresses, links, phone numbers and extra spaces from any text. Copy the clean result or download it as a text file.

    Open Text Cleaner

  • Text Encryptor and Decryptor

    Lock your text with a password so only people who know the password can read it. Unlock it again on the same page.

    Open Text Encryptor and Decryptor

  • DOCX to PDF and Reader

    Open a Word .docx file and read it in your browser, save it as a PDF, or download it as plain text.

    Open DOCX to PDF and Reader

  • PDF to Text Converter

    Pull all the text out of a PDF and save it as a plain text file. Works on digital PDFs, not scanned photos of pages.

    Open PDF to Text Converter

  • DOCX to Text Converter

    Pull all the text out of a Word document and save it as a plain text file.

    Open DOCX to Text Converter

  • DOCX to Web Page Converter

    Turn a Word document into a web page file that keeps headings, lists and tables.

    Open DOCX to Web Page Converter

  • Text to DOCX Converter

    Type or paste plain text and download it as a Word .docx document.

    Open Text to DOCX Converter

  • AI Studio

    Ask AI anything in plain language and hear the answer read aloud. Our first AI tool, Ask AI, is ready.

    Open AI Studio

  • Audio Books Coming soon

    Free audio books that you can listen to online or download.

    Learn about Audio Books

  • Audio Library Coming soon

    A collection of useful audio recordings, tutorials and learning material.

    Learn about the Audio Library

  • PDF Manipulation

    Read, split, merge, resize, compress, rotate and organize PDFs, add page numbers and watermarks, and convert between PDF, images and text.

    Open PDF Manipulation

  • Documents Converter

    Change documents between formats in your browser: PDF to text, text to PDF, image to PDF, PDF to image, text to DOCX, DOCX to text, DOCX to web page and DOCX to PDF.

    Open Documents Converter

  • PDF Reader

    Open any PDF and read its text on the screen, listen to it aloud, or save it as a text file.

    Open PDF Reader

  • PDF Split

    Take out the pages you need from a PDF and save them as a new PDF file.

    Open PDF Split

  • PDF Merge

    Join two or more PDF files into one single PDF, in the order you choose.

    Open PDF Merge

  • PDF to Image Converter

    Turn each page of a PDF into a picture (PNG image) that you can download and share.

    Open PDF to Image Converter

  • Image to PDF Converter

    Turn your photos and pictures into one PDF file, one picture per page.

    Open Image to PDF Converter

  • Text to PDF Converter

    Type or paste text in any language, including Hindi, and save it as a PDF file.

    Open Text to PDF Converter

  • PDF Resize

    Change the page size of a PDF: fit pages to A4, A5, A3, Letter or Legal, or scale them by a percentage.

    Open PDF Resize

  • PDF Compress

    Make a PDF file smaller so it is easier to share. Choose between the smallest size or keeping text readable for screen readers.

    Open PDF Compress

  • PDF Rotate

    Turn pages of a PDF by 90, 180 or 270 degrees: all pages, odd or even pages, or a range you choose.

    Open PDF Rotate

  • PDF Organize Pages

    See every page of a PDF, change their order and delete the ones you do not need, then save the result.

    Open PDF Organize Pages

  • PDF Page Numbers

    Add page numbers to a PDF at the top or bottom, in the corner or the middle.

    Open PDF Page Numbers

  • PDF Watermark

    Stamp text like Draft or Confidential diagonally across every page of a PDF.

    Open PDF Watermark

  • PDF Details Editor

    See and change the hidden details of a PDF, like its title, author, subject and keywords.

    Open PDF Details Editor

  • Government Circulars

    Official disability rights, banking and accessibility PDF downloads with short English descriptions.

    Learn about Government Circulars

  • Currency Converter

    Change an amount from one currency to another, for example rupees to dollars, euros or pounds.

    Open Currency Converter

  • QR Code for Text or a Link

    Type any text or web address, and the tool makes a QR code for it. Anyone who scans the code sees your text, or goes straight to your link.

    Open QR Code for Text or a Link

  • QR Code for a Wi-Fi Network

    Make a QR code that connects a phone to your Wi-Fi when scanned, so guests do not have to type the password. Your network name and password never leave this page.

    Open QR Code for a Wi-Fi Network

  • QR Code for a Phone Number

    Make a QR code that starts a call to your number when someone scans it. Type the number with the country code, for example +91 followed by your ten digits.

    Open QR Code for a Phone Number

  • QR Code for UPI Payments

    Make a QR code for your UPI ID, with an optional amount and note, so people can pay you by scanning it with any UPI app. The code works for any UPI ID, like Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm.

    Open QR Code for UPI Payments

  • QR Code for an Email Address

    Make a QR code that opens a new email to your address, with an optional subject and message typed ready to send.

    Open QR Code for an Email Address

  • QR Code for a Text Message

    Make a QR code that opens a new text message to your number, with an optional message typed ready to send.

    Open QR Code for a Text Message

  • QR Code for a WhatsApp Chat

    Make a QR code that opens a WhatsApp chat with your number, with an optional first message ready to send.

    Open QR Code for a WhatsApp Chat

  • QR Code for a Contact Card

    Make a QR code that saves your name, phone number, email and organisation to a phone as a new contact.

    Open QR Code for a Contact Card

  • Audio Trimmer

    Keep only the part of a recording you need, or remove a part from the middle. You type the times in seconds, so it works fully with a keyboard.

    Open Audio Trimmer

  • Audio Merger

    Join two or more audio files into one single file. After choosing your files you can change their order with the Move up and Move down buttons, and add a short silence between them.

    Open Audio Merger

  • Audio Converter

    Change an audio file into MP3 or WAV. You can also choose a video file here: its sound will be taken out and saved as audio.

    Open Audio Converter

  • Volume Changer

    Make a recording louder or quieter. For example, 50 makes it half as loud and 200 makes it twice as loud. There is also an option to make the recording as loud as possible without distortion.

    Open Volume Changer

  • Speed Changer

    Make a recording faster or slower while voices still sound natural, without turning into chipmunks or monsters. For example, 150 plays the recording in two thirds of the time.

    Open Speed Changer

  • Voice Over Mixer

    Put your voice recording on top of background music. The music automatically becomes quieter while your voice plays, and louder again when your voice ends. This is perfect for making a voice over for a video or a presentation.

    Open Voice Over Mixer

  • Multi-Track Mixer

    Play up to four audio files together, all starting at the same time, each with its own loudness. The result is as long as the longest file.

    Open Multi-Track Mixer

  • Fade In and Fade Out

    Make a recording start and end smoothly instead of beginning or stopping suddenly.

    Open Fade In and Fade Out

  • Audio Reverser

    Turn a recording around so it plays backwards, from the end to the beginning.

    Open Audio Reverser

  • Clear Phone Speaker

    If water or dust has entered your phone's speaker and the sound feels muffled, this tool plays a special cleaning tone that shakes the speaker and pushes the water or dust out. Hold the phone with the speaker facing down while it plays.

    Open Clear Phone Speaker

  • Text Speaker

    Reads any text out loud with the voices built into your phone or computer, with your choice of voice, speed and pitch.

    Open Text Speaker

  • Text to Image Creator

    Type a few words describing a picture and the tool creates the image, ready to download. It makes still pictures only, not videos.

    Open Text to Image Creator

  • Video Trimmer

    Keep only the part of a video between two times you type, in seconds.

    Open Video Trimmer

  • Video to Audio

    Take the sound out of a video and save it as an audio file. Useful for songs, speeches and lectures you have as video.

    Open Video to Audio

  • Video Compressor

    Make a video smaller so it is easier to send or upload. Type the exact size you want, and the tool works out the right quality. Making a video smaller always lowers its quality a little; the smaller you go, the more you will notice.

    Open Video Compressor

  • Video Format Converter

    Save a video in a new format. WebM works in every modern browser. MP4, which plays almost everywhere, is offered when your browser knows how to make it; if the MP4 choice is missing, your browser can only make WebM. Most MP4, WebM and phone MOV files can be read.

    Open Video Format Converter

  • Video Speed Changer

    Make a video faster or slower. The sound stays natural. For example, 200 plays the video in half the time, and 50 makes it twice as long.

    Open Video Speed Changer

  • Remove Sound

    Make a silent copy of a video, with the picture exactly as it was. It is saved in WebM format and the work happens in real time.

    Open Remove Sound

  • Merge Videos

    Join two or more videos into one, in the order you choose. For a clean join, the videos should have the same width and height; if they are very different, the tool will tell you honestly instead of making a broken file.

    Open Merge Videos

  • Slideshow Creator

    Turn your photos into a video. Each photo is shown for the number of seconds you choose. You can add a music or voice file that plays through the whole video. The video is made in WebM format, which plays in all modern browsers and on phones. Making it happens in real time.

    Open Slideshow Creator

  • Take a Photo from a Video

    Save any moment of a video as a picture. Type the second you want, and the tool takes that frame out as a photo.

    Open Take a Photo from a Video

  • Image Editor

    Edit your photo step by step below. When you are done, you can make it an exact file size in the Image Compressor section, or turn it into a passport photo in the Passport Size Photo section.

    Open Image Editor

  • Image Format Converter

    Change a photo from one file type to another: JPG, PNG or WebP. For example, turn a PNG into a JPG when a form only accepts JPG photos.

    Open Image Format Converter

  • Merge Photos (join photos into one)

    Join two or more photos into a single photo: side by side (left to right) or stacked (top to bottom). Useful for before-and-after photos or for putting two pictures on one print.

    Open Merge Photos (join photos into one)

  • Split a Photo into Parts

    Cut one photo into smaller parts, for example to print a big photo on small papers, or to post one photo as a grid. You choose how many rows and columns; each part downloads as its own file.

    Open Split a Photo into Parts

  • Image Compressor

    This free tool makes your photo fit an exact file size that you type, for example 20 KB, 50 KB or 1 MB. It is made for government forms, exam forms and job applications that say "photo must be less than 50 KB" and so on.

    Open Image Compressor

  • Passport Size Photo Maker

    This free tool turns any photo of you into a correct passport size photo, with the exact dimensions used for Indian passports, visas, exam forms and job applications. It runs fully inside your browser: your photo never leaves your device.

    Open Passport Size Photo Maker

  • Circle Crop (profile photo)

    Cut your photo into a circle, or into a square with rounded corners. This is popular for profile photos on WhatsApp, Telegram and other apps. The part outside the circle becomes see-through (transparent).

    Open Circle Crop (profile photo)

  • Remove White Background

    Make the white background of a photo see-through. This is made for signatures and logos scanned or photographed on white paper: sign on white paper, take a photo, and this tool gives you a signature with a see-through background that you can put on any document.

    Open Remove White Background

  • Colour Finder (hear the colours of a photo)

    This tool looks at your photo and tells you its main colours in plain words, with the exact colour codes. It is made for blind and low-vision users who want to know what a photo looks like, and for anyone matching colours for a design.

    Open Colour Finder (hear the colours of a photo)

  • Ask Aloud

    Type a question in plain words and get a short spoken answer. Answers can be wrong, so check important things.

    Open Ask Aloud
