All Tools

Here are all the tools and sections available on Vaibhav Tech Vision. Everything is free and fully accessible with a screen reader and keyboard. Some sections are still being built, and they are honestly marked as coming soon. Choose a section below to open it.

  • Text Manipulation

    Change letter case, count words and characters, clean messy text and lock your text with a password.

    Open Text Manipulation

  • AI Studio

    Ask AI anything in plain language and hear the answer read aloud. Our first AI tool, Ask AI, is ready.

    Open AI Studio

  • Audio Books Coming soon

    Free audio books that you can listen to online or download.

    Learn about Audio Books

  • Audio Library Coming soon

    A collection of useful audio recordings, tutorials and learning material.

    Learn about the Audio Library

  • PDF Manipulation

    Read, split, merge, resize, compress, rotate and organize PDFs, add page numbers and watermarks, and convert between PDF, images and text.

    Open PDF Manipulation

  • Government Circulars

    Official disability rights, banking and accessibility PDF downloads with short English descriptions.

    Learn about Government Circulars

  • Currency Converter

    Change an amount from one currency to another, for example rupees to dollars, euros or pounds.

    Open Currency Converter

  • Image Editor

    Make a photo bigger or smaller, crop it, rotate or flip it, fix brightness and colours, add text, and save it as JPG, PNG or WebP.

    Open Image Editor

  • Audio Editor

    Trim or cut a recording, join audio files into one, convert to MP3 or WAV, change volume and speed, mix your voice over music, mix tracks, add fades, reverse audio, and clear your phone speaker.

    Open Audio Editor

  • Video Editor

    Trim a video, take its sound out as MP3, compress to a size you choose, convert format, change speed, remove sound, join videos, turn photos into a slideshow video, and save any video moment as a photo.

    Open Video Editor
