PDF Split

This tool takes out the pages you choose from a PDF and saves them as a new PDF file. For example, if your PDF has 50 pages and you only need pages 10 to 15, this tool makes a new PDF with just those pages.

Choose a PDF file, type the first and last page you want, and press Split PDF. The file stays inside your browser; it is never uploaded anywhere.
