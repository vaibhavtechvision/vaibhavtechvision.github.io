Accessibility Statement

Accessibility is the first rule of Vaibhav Tech Vision, not an afterthought. This website is built for blind and visually impaired users from the very beginning.

What We Do

  • Every page has a skip link, one clear main heading and a simple structure.
  • Every page works fully with a keyboard, without a mouse.
  • Pages are tested with screen readers like TalkBack and NVDA.
  • Text is written in simple language, and colours have strong contrast.
  • Forms and tools announce their results to screen readers.

Feedback

If any part of this website is difficult for you to use, please tell us through the comments on our YouTube channel. We take accessibility problems seriously and will try to fix them quickly.
